Declaration and consent — credit bureau enquiry

  1. I confirm that the information provided in this form is true, correct and complete.
  2. I authorise Pro Mortgage SA (“Pro Mortgage”) to request my consumer credit report and credit information from registered credit bureaux, including Experian, for the purpose of assessing my eligibility for home-loan pre-qualification and origination, being a permissible purpose in terms of the National Credit Act 34 of 2005.
  3. I consent to Pro Mortgage processing the personal information in this form for that purpose in terms of the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013, and to Pro Mortgage sharing it with participating banks and credit providers solely for the purpose of originating a home loan on my behalf.
  4. I understand that this enquiry may be recorded on my credit profile as a consumer-consent enquiry.
  5. I may withdraw this consent in writing at any time before the enquiry is performed.
